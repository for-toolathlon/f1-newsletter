## Race-day weather
Cool and dry at Silverstone: air temps ranged from 14.5°C to 37.6°C, track temperatures from 20.7°C to 37.9°C, humidity 60–78%, pressure 991.7–992.6 hPa, light winds (0.5–2.5 m/s), and very little rain (0–1 mm) throughout the race.

## The win
For a F1 fan club newsletter, the highlight: Hamilton winning his home race at Silverstone — 25 points from pole position, his 5th British GP win of the calendar year and the final nail in his championship title run.
